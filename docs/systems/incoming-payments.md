# Incoming payments (Stripe payments with no label — "Payments to confirm")
_Last verified against code: 2026-10-23b — Claude (dev job `40a58d9a`, SANDBOX): **Phase 2 added — the CRM lead page's "Create payment link"** (see its section). Earlier 2026-10-23: Phase 1 (Antonio: Christian Ranieri's €397 paid-call payment converted his lead and was never recorded), browser-tested on sandbox. Neither phase is on production._

## What it is
Money that arrives through a Stripe link **that says nothing about itself** (a link made by hand in the Stripe Dashboard, e.g. the three "Strategic Call" links) used to be treated by the webhook as a client service sale: it flipped the lead to `Converted` by email match and tried to insert a `payments` row (which fails silently when the payer has no contact or account). Now such a payment **changes nothing by itself** and waits in the Calendar, tab **Payments to confirm**, until staff say what it was.

## Business rules
- **Labelled = ours.** A completed Checkout Session is "unlabelled" only when its metadata carries none of `source`, `payment_kind`, `offer_token`, `invoice_number`, `lead_id` (`isUnlabelledPayment`, `lib/incoming-payments/classify.ts`). Every session our code creates carries `source: td-operations…` (offer/invoice checkout in `lib/stripe-checkout.ts`; autopay setup sessions are mode `setup`, never money) or `payment_kind` (marketing links). Verified 2026-10-23 against `webhook_events`: the only unlabelled payments since August were Ranieri, Germani, Anastasio, Stellacci (all hand-made `plink_…` links).
- **The webhook** (`app/api/webhooks/stripe/route.ts`, step 1c, right after the marketing branch): unlabelled → keep the saved `webhook_events` row (the record), skip EVERYTHING else (no lead conversion, no email lookup, no tier change, no pending-activation consumption, no payment row, no invoice reconcile). If the event row cannot be saved (any error but a duplicate) it throws → Stripe retries, so a payment can never silently vanish.
- **The waiting list is derived, not stored** (`loadWaitingPayments`, `lib/incoming-payments/load.ts`): saved `checkout.session.completed` events since `INCOMING_PAYMENTS_SINCE` (2026-10-01) that are unlabelled and paid, minus sessions in `incoming_payment_resolutions`, minus any payment that already has a `payments.stripe_payment_id` equal to its payment-intent or session id. That is why payments that arrived BEFORE this existed show up with no backfill. The cut-off keeps older payments (settled by hand or through the bank feed — e.g. Stellacci's €257, recorded 2026-09-18) from reappearing.
- **Three choices** (`confirmIncomingPayment`, `lib/incoming-payments/confirm.ts`; route `POST /api/crm/admin-actions/incoming-payments/confirm`, permission `confirm_payment`):
  1. **Paid call** — `recordPaidCall` (see `referrals-circleback.md`): a Paid invoice "Paid Strategy Call — <date>" + a person-scoped credit note (deductible from the next offer).
  2. **Paid call — already deducted by hand** — same, but the credit is born used (`creditUsedAtCreation`, "revenue only, history kept"): use it when the amount was already taken off an offer manually, so the system never deducts it twice.
  3. **Already recorded / not a client payment** — writes nothing to the books; a note (≥3 chars) is required; the payment leaves the list.
- The money (amount, currency, payer, Stripe ids) is **re-read server-side from the saved event**; the browser only sends the session id, the choice, an optional lead id and a note. Idempotent: the invoice/credit are keyed `paid-call-manual:stripe-session:<id>:…`, the resolution table's primary key is the session id.
- **A lead is never converted** and no client record is tagged (no `converted_to_*`, no `existing_client_contact_id`): a paid call is not a sale (R094). The lead only gets a dated line in its notes. The contact is found/created by EMAIL (the picked lead's email, else the payer's — never the card holder's name).
- **Marketing:** a waiting payment whose email is also a TD Marketing lead shows a warning to NOT record it as a paid call (marketing sales follow marketing's own money rules). Phase 3 will add a real "Marketing sale" choice. Marketing's own links carry `payment_kind` and never reach this list.

## Phase 2 — "Create payment link" on the lead page (sandbox, 2026-10-23)
- **Where:** the lead page's Actions card, button "Create payment link" (`components/incoming-payments/create-payment-link.tsx`, shown for every lead). **Free** amount (plain number, comma or dot, > 0, ≤ 50,000; above 2,000 a tick is required and the route enforces it too), currency EUR/USD (Italian-language leads default to EUR), a free description (what the client sees on the Stripe page; default "Strategic call", ≤ 80 chars), and a purpose.
- **Purpose `paid_call`** (default): the Checkout Session carries `payment_kind: paid_call` (+ `crm_lead_ref` = the lead, `client_email`). When paid, the Stripe webhook (non-client branch, BEFORE the redelivery check like the marketing ledger) calls `recordPaidCallFromSession` → the shared `settlePayment` core: Paid "Strategic Call" invoice + a live credit, by itself, no confirmation; the lead is never converted; one dated note line on the lead. A failure throws → 500 → Stripe retries; a deleted lead falls back to the payer's email.
- **Purpose `other`:** carries NO label key (only `crm_lead_ref` / `client_email`, which are not labels), so when paid it lands in Payments to confirm like any unlabelled payment. The link keys `source`, `offer_token`, `invoice_number`, `lead_id` are NEVER used on these links (a test pins it): they would make the payment look like one of our own sales.
- **Route:** `POST /api/crm/admin-actions/payment-links/create` (permission `create_offer`). Uses `createMarketingCheckoutSession` (card only, **23-hour expiry** — Stripe's checkout limit is 24h; shown on screen). Nothing is sent to the client: staff copy the link. The lead gets a dated note line ("payment link created by … (paid call: …)").
- **Pure rules:** `lib/incoming-payments/payment-link.ts` (`parseLinkRequest`, `parseAmountCents`, `linkMetadata`, `CONFIRM_ABOVE`); `PAYMENT_KIND_PAID_CALL` in `lib/payments/payment-kind.ts`.
- **Proof (sandbox, 2026-10-23):** real Stripe TEST-mode link created from the browser (metadata verified on the session via the Stripe API); the paid event simulated through the real recorder, run THREE times: one invoice + one live credit (397), lead still "Call Done", one confirmation note. Sandbox webhooks are blocked by design, so the end-to-end Stripe→webhook hop is covered by unit tests only.
- **Found and fixed on the way:** (1) `recordPaidCall` threw a duplicate-key error on a SECOND run for the same payment once its credit was activated, because `createTDInvoice`'s idempotency lookup skips status "Credit" (on its dead-invoice list) — this also affected Calendly redelivery. It now reuses an activated credit with that key and never re-activates (`tests/unit/paid-call-credit-redelivery.test.ts`). (2) `createMarketingCheckoutSession` now trims `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL`: the sandbox value ends in a stray newline and Stripe answered "Not a valid URL" (offer checkout, `createCheckoutSession`, still reads it untrimmed — check the Vercel value before relying on it in sandbox).

## How it's built
- Files: `lib/incoming-payments/{classify,load,confirm,payment-link,link-payment}.ts`, `components/incoming-payments/{payments-tab,confirm-actions}.tsx`, route above; wiring in `lib/new-calendar/params.ts` (tab `payments`), `app/(dashboard)/new-calendar/page.tsx` (reads the list on EVERY tab for the red count; tolerant — if unreadable the Calendar still opens and the payments tab says "Could not load", never "nothing waiting"), `components/new-calendar/new-calendar-view.tsx`.
- Table `incoming_payment_resolutions` (migration `20261023-1200-incoming-payment-resolutions.sql` + `.UNDO.sql`; RLS on, no policy = server only).
- `recordPaidCall` got two backward-compatible options: `payment.paymentIntentId` (stamp directly instead of resolving a charge id) and `manual.sourceLabel` (note wording).
- The Calendar's read-only test (`new-calendar-wiring`) still holds: the writing UI lives in `components/incoming-payments/`, not in `components/new-calendar/`.

## Gotchas, invariants & past bugs
- The sandbox database has no `marketing_leads` table; the payer lookup tolerates ONLY "table does not exist" (PGRST205 / 42P01) — any other error stops the list.
- Phase 1 cannot process an unlabelled payment that was really a client SERVICE payment: do that with the lead's Confirm Payment as before (Phase 3 adds it here).
- Monica Germani (2026-10-23, €397, same link) is in no lead, contact or marketing record: she stays waiting until the Marketing choice (Phase 3) exists; do not dismiss her as "recorded elsewhere".
- Not built yet: Phase 3 (Marketing sale + client service choices), a What's New line.
