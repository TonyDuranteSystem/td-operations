## What it is
Antonio asked for a place on the Contact where he can see the contract the client SIGNED after reading the offer (an offer is only what we offered and sent), click it open, see the installment amounts the system saved at signing, and fix them when they differ from the contract (typically a "." versus "," mix-up). The card reads straight from where the signed files already are — **nothing is copied or moved**.

## Business rules (all approved on the mockup, 2026-11-13)
- **One line per signed OFFER**, newest signing first. If the same offer was signed more than once (12 offers on production have 2-3 `contracts` rows within about a day), only the newest file is listed.
- **A contract shows on a contact** when its offer was made for that person (`offers.contact_id`), was sent to their email (`offers.client_email`, case-insensitive), was made from a lead that became that person (`leads.converted_to_contact_id`), or belongs to a company they are linked to (`offers.account_id` through `account_contacts`). So an annual renewal (it belongs to the company) shows on EVERY person linked to the company.
- **The amounts are shown exactly as saved** — never rounded, never tidied (a saved `2.5001` shows `2.5001`; a saved `200010001000` shows `200,010,001,000`). The yearly figure is the saved `annual_fee`, not January + June, so a record that disagrees with itself is visible. The line does not compare the saved amounts with the PDF (Antonio does that himself); since 2026-11-15 it DOES compare them with the company's own installment amounts — see "Company check".
- **Where the amounts come from:** formation / onboarding → `contracts.annual_fee` + `contracts.installments` (`{"jan":n,"jun":n}`, written by the browser signing page). Annual renewal → `annual_agreements.cost_summary` (text such as "$1,000"; the 62 renewal `contracts` rows save NO amount). Tax return, ITIN, closure, name change → "no installment amounts for this contract".
- **Edit is OWNER-ONLY** (`isOwnerOnly`: Antonio, plus `NEXT_PUBLIC_EXTRA_OWNER_EMAILS`). Others see the card without the Edit button, and the route refuses them (403). Open is for all staff (`isStaffUser`); a client, a partner or a signed-out visitor is refused.
- **Both ways of writing an amount are read**: "1,250" and "1.250" are both 1250 (a separator followed by exactly three digits, with 1-3 digits before it, is a thousands mark; one or two digits after it is a decimal). The form shows "Read as: …" before Save; anything else is refused rather than guessed; above 1,000,000 is refused (a glued number).
- **Edit changes the saved amount only.** NEVER the signed PDF, never an invoice already sent, never the company's own installment amounts (`accounts.installment_1/2_amount` — the June cron `annual-installments` uses THOSE, verified in the route's header and select list). For a renewal it rewrites the agreement's `cost_summary` in the layout it already has, and the screen warns that the client's renewal agreement page shows those amounts (`renewal-agreement.tsx` reads `offer.cost_summary`).
- **Every correction is kept**: an `action_log` row (`action_type = signed_contract_amounts_corrected`, `record_id` = the signing record's id for both kinds) with the old text, the new text, both raw values and who. The card shows "Corrected by … on … · was …" from the newest one.
- **No personal data reaches the browser**: the loader selects only the columns the card needs — never `client_passport`, the address or the storage path.

## Company check (2026-11-15, dev job fba44df5 — Antonio: "the CRM says something else, the system should tell me the mismatch")
- **Why:** four places hold "the amount" and nothing links them after signing: the offer (`offers.recurring_costs` / `cost_summary`), the signed contract (`contracts.annual_fee` + `installments`), the company (`accounts.installment_1_amount` / `installment_2_amount` + their currencies — what the CRM shows and what the June `annual-installments` invoice is raised from), and the payment rows. The helper that copies an offer's installments onto the company (`lib/operations/onboarding-account-upgrade.ts`, since 2026-05-06) fills a column ONLY when it is empty and writes no history when nothing changes, so a company created earlier (or by hand) can keep an old value for good. Production 2026-11-15 (read-only): of 44 signed contracts with amounts, 35 matched their company, 1 differed (the contract said 1,175 + 1,175, the company 1,000 + 1,000), 8 offers were not filed on any company. Antonio decided the SIGNED amount is right; correcting the company's amounts is his own step and the check never writes anything.
- **What the line says**, under the grey box, for formation and onboarding contracts only (renewals keep their amounts as text on the agreement and are NOT compared; tax return / ITIN / closure / name change have no installment amounts):
  - **match** — quiet green "Matches the company's installment amounts in the CRM."
  - **differs** — amber "Differs from the company. The company's installment amounts in the CRM are January $x · June $y." (the COMPANY's figures, as saved, never rounded). Any of: January differs, June differs, or the currency differs when BOTH sides say which.
  - **company empty** — amber: the company has no installment amounts saved.
  - **not compared** (grey, so a missing note can never be read as "matches"): the offer is not filed on a company (or that company is not found); or the contract's own amounts cannot be read as January and June.
  - **could not compare** (grey): the company lookup failed. A failed lookup never hides a contract and never says match.
- **Which company:** the company the contract's OFFER is filed on (`offers.account_id`) — never guessed from the person. A person linked to several companies gets each line compared with its own. An offer with no company shows "not compared".
- **After an Edit:** the corrected line is re-read through the same loader, so the note is recomputed (browser-verified: Edit 1,175 → 1,000 against a company holding 1,000 turned the amber note into the green match). While the Edit form is open, or when a save could not be read back, no note is shown.
- **Nothing is fixed by this.** The Edit still changes only the contract's saved amount; the company's amounts are corrected on the company, by a person.
- **Where it lives:** pure rule `lib/signed-contracts/company-check.ts` (`checkContractAgainstCompany`), wired in `entries.ts` (optional `companies` input; absent = no comparison) and `load.ts` (`lookUpCompanies`: one read of the installment columns of the companies of the signed formation/onboarding offers; a failed read is reported as `lookup_failed`, not thrown), drawn by `CompanyCheckNote` in `components/contacts/signed-contracts-card.tsx`. Tests: `tests/unit/signed-contracts-company-check.test.ts` and the extended `signed-contracts-load.test.ts`.
- **Not covered, on purpose:** the company page's Signed contracts card (it shows no amounts by design); renewal agreements; a standing all-clients report. A company whose price legitimately changed after signing will stay amber until the company or the contract is brought in line — that is the intent (it only tells).

## On the Contacts list (2026-11-15, dev job 02dbf9d3 — Antonio: "when there is an amber message for a mismatch I want the contact to be amber with an hover to click and read the mismatch instead of clicking on the contact")
- **What it does:** on `/contacts` a person is flagged when ANY of their signed contracts would draw an amber note on their page — the two amber findings only: **differs** and **company has no amounts**. Grey cases (not compared, could not compare) flag nobody. The flagged name is amber (`text-amber-700`) with a `TriangleAlert` icon and a screen-reader line "Check the contract amounts" (`data-testid="contract-mismatch-name"`); amber is already used on that row (the "onboarding" chip), so the icon is part of the signal, not colour alone. The name's existing hover card (`personCard`) gets an amber block at the top (`CardModel.alert`, drawn by `HoverCardBody`, `data-testid="card-alert"`): a heading, then per contract "<Company> · <Formation|Onboarding> contract / Contract says: January $x · June $y / Company in the CRM: January $x · June $y" (or "The company has no installment amounts saved in the CRM."). More than 4 findings are cut to "+ N more — open the contact". The card is the same interactive, copyable card as before.
- **Same rule as the card, not a second one.** `lib/signed-contracts/list-mismatches.ts` (`contractMismatchesByPerson`, pure) runs the card's own `buildEntries` (newest file per offer, same reading of the saved amounts, same `checkContractAgainstCompany`) and then attaches each flagged offer to people by the card's own four ways: `offers.contact_id`; `offers.client_email` (case-insensitive); the offer's lead (`leads.converted_to_contact_id`); the offer's company through `account_contacts`. Consequence, same as on the card: one company with a mismatch flags EVERY person linked to it; a person with several companies gets one block per differing contract.
- **One bulk read, not the card's per-person lookup** (`loadContractMismatchesForPeople` in `load.ts`, called from `app/(dashboard)/contacts/page.tsx` inside the page's existing `Promise.all`, for the 50 people on the page): all formation + onboarding offers (about 105 rows on production, paged with `fetchAllRows`), their contracts in batches of 100 tokens, the leads of the page's people, and the companies' installment columns (`lookUpCompanies`). Named columns only (never the passport/address columns of `contracts`); the client email is used only to match and never reaches the page. Service-role client, like the Contact page's loader. The first read of people now also selects `email`.
- **A failed read says so.** Any failed read returns `{ kind: 'failed' }` and the page header shows "Could not check the contract amounts just now, so no contact is marked. Reload the page to try again." (`data-testid="contract-check-failed"`) — never an empty answer that looks like "all fine".
- **Not changed, on purpose:** the Address book (standalone, reads the CRM read-only), chat/tasks/calendar name links. (The Accounts list and the company page were left out here and added the same day by dev job `a2a663cf` — next section; the Accounts list's *Main contact* hover is still plain.) No dismiss button: nothing clears the amber except correcting the company or the contract (a company that was legitimately repriced stays amber until then — Antonio's call whether to add an "It's correct" button like the one on "How they became our client"). Hover cards open with a mouse only, so on a phone the name is amber but no card opens.
- **Verified (sandbox browser, made-up data, then removed):** 7 test contacts — differs, company shared by a second person, company with no amounts, matching, two companies both differing (two blocks), offer reached only by email, offer with no company — flagged exactly as designed at desktop and phone width, cards read back by script. Production on 2026-11-15 (read-only, counselor): exactly 1 differing contract, 0 company-empty.
- **Tests:** `tests/unit/signed-contracts-list-mismatches.test.ts` (rule, four ways, parity with the card), `signed-contracts-list-load.test.ts` (bulk read, batches, failures), `crm-lists-contract-alert.test.ts` (card text + render).

## On the Accounts list and the company page (2026-11-15, dev job a2a663cf — Antonio: "the amber should go on the account as well, above all if the amber is related to the company … it must dismiss after fixing the issue")
- **What it does:** a company whose signed formation / onboarding contract has the amber finding (**differs** or **company has no amounts**) gets (1) on `/accounts`: an amber name with a `TriangleAlert` icon and a screen-reader line (`data-testid="contract-mismatch-name"`) whose hover card carries the same amber block as the Contacts list (title = the contract alone, since the company is already the card title; "+ N more / Open the company to see them." when long); (2) on `/accounts/[id]`: an amber note (`components/accounts/contract-mismatch-note.tsx`, `data-testid="contract-mismatch-note"`) at the top of the **Annual Installments** card, with the contract's January/June, the company's January/June and one line saying to correct whichever side is wrong. A company that is NOT a Client (One-Time has one signed contract on production) has no installments card, so the same note is drawn on its own in that place.
- **Which company:** the one the offer is **filed on** (`offers.account_id`) — the very company the check compared the contract with. `contractMismatchesByCompany` (pure, `list-mismatches.ts`) reuses the shared `flaggedFindings` step (the card's own `buildEntries`), the same step `contractMismatchesByPerson` uses, so the Contact page, the Contacts list, the Accounts list and the company page cannot disagree. An offer with no company flags nobody (grey "not compared" on the card). The finding does not say which side is wrong (the contract may be the one with the mistake) — it shows both numbers.
- **Reads (`loadContractMismatchesForCompanies`, `load.ts`):** only the formation + onboarding offers filed on the companies of the page (50 on the list, 1 on the company page; batches of 100), their contracts (batches of 100 tokens, named columns only), and the companies' installment columns (`lookUpCompanies`). Any failed read returns `{ kind: 'failed' }`: the Accounts header says "Could not check the contract amounts just now, so no company is marked. Reload the page to try again." (`data-testid="contract-check-failed"`) and the company page says it grey in the card — never silence that looks like "all fine".
- **No dismiss button, by Antonio's word.** The check is run again every time the page loads, so the amber is gone as soon as the amounts agree. On the Accounts list the amount cells now ask the server for the page again after a save (`router.refresh()` after the override), so correcting an amount on the row ends the amber on that row without a manual reload. On the company page a saved amount shows after the next load (the page already works that way for its other fields).
- **Not covered, on purpose:** renewal agreements (kept on the agreement), a standing all-clients report, locked / closed companies are NOT suppressed (same rule on every screen; none exists on production).
- **Tests:** `tests/unit/signed-contracts-company-mismatches.test.ts` (rule by company, parity with the person side, loader reads and failures, company card alert, the note).

## How it's built
- **Pure logic (unit-tested, client-safe where noted):** `lib/signed-contracts/amount-input.ts` (read "1,250"/"1.250", `formatNumber`, `formatSavedNumber` — client-safe), `contract-amounts.ts` (read/display/build the contract's amounts), `renewal-amounts.ts` (read/rewrite the agreement's `cost_summary`: two real layouts — TWO groups "First Installment (January)" / "Second Installment (June)", or ONE group "<year> Payment Schedule" with an item per installment — anything else is shown as lines and not editable), `labels.ts` (chip + the real agreement titles), `entries.ts` (rows → lines: newest per offer, currency symbol only when the offer says which, `hasUsablePath`), `types.ts`.
- **Server:** `lib/signed-contracts/load.ts` (`loadSignedContractsForContact`, `loadSignedContractEntry`), `lib/signed-contracts/correct.ts` (`correctSignedContractAmounts`).
- **Routes:** `GET /api/crm/signed-contracts/[id]/open` → `{url}` (staff; signs the path RECORDED on `contracts.pdf_path` for 60 s via `createRecordedSignedUrl`, never a folder listing) and `POST /api/crm/signed-contracts/[id]/amounts` `{january, june, expectedUpdatedAt}` → `{entry, warning?}` (owner; `entry` is `null`, with a warning, when the amounts were saved but the line could not be read back; a body that is not an object is refused 400). `[id]` = `contracts.id`.
- **Where the lines are held:** `components/contacts/contact-detail.tsx` keeps them in state (`replaceEntry` in `lib/signed-contracts/list.ts`) and the card is controlled (`entries` + `onEntryCorrected`). The Overview tab is dropped when another tab opens, so state kept inside the card showed the OLD amounts again on coming back (and a second Edit then got 409 from the person's own first edit). Browser-verified: edit → Services tab → Overview shows the corrected amounts and the "Corrected by…" line, and a second Edit saves.
- **Screen:** `components/contacts/signed-contracts-card.tsx`, rendered by `components/contacts/contact-detail.tsx` (Overview tab, in the same grid cell as the Offers panel so they stay together); the data is loaded in `app/(dashboard)/contacts/[id]/page.tsx` and a failure shows a plain note in the card, never a broken page.
- **Safety of the write:** both amounts are parsed on the server with the same function the form uses; the update is matched on the `updated_at` the person saw (`.eq`, or `.is null`), so two people cannot overwrite each other (409 "changed by someone else… reload"); `contracts` has no trigger, so the code sets `updated_at` itself. The contract row written is the newest one for the offer (the line the person is looking at).
- **Open button:** opens a blank tab inside the click, then points it at the private link; if the browser blocks the tab, a "Click here to open the signed contract" link appears (works for a minute); a missing file turns the line amber ("File can't be opened right now").
- **Failure behaviour (never hide, never mislabel):** `load.ts` throws with the real cause when the company-link, lead, offers, contracts or agreements read fails — a person with signed contracts must never see "No signed contract yet" because a lookup timed out (the page shows the error in the card). `correct.ts` reports `ok` once the amounts are written, even if the read-back fails; if the history row cannot be written the warning carries the old amount and the full record goes to `console.error`; reads of the offer / agreement surface their own error (500) instead of a false 404.
- **Tests:** `tests/unit/signed-contracts-amount-input.test.ts`, `-amounts.test.ts`, `-entries.test.ts`, `-routes.test.ts` (route guards, the exact written shape, the old value kept, the 409, renewal rewrite, nothing else written, read-back failure, history-row failure), `-load.test.ts` (the four visibility rules, lookup failures throw, only needed columns read), `-list.test.ts`.

## Gotchas, invariants & past bugs
- **Why the card exists / the mistakes it makes visible.** The signing page computes `annual_fee` / `installments` IN THE BROWSER with `parseFloat(String(raw).replace(/[^0-9.]/g,''))` (`app/offer/[token]/contract/page.tsx` ~663-702, also `service-agreement.tsx`); `sanitizeContractFields` (`lib/offers/public-signing.ts`) only checks the shape. A one-line offer cost like "$2,000/year ($1,000 Jan + $1,000 Jun)" becomes `200010001000`, and an Italian "$2.500/anno ($1.250 gen + $1.250 giu)" becomes `2.5001`. The PDF prints the original text, so the saved record can differ from the contract. **This signing-page bug is NOT fixed by this card** (Antonio chose to see and correct, not to have it investigated); an offer with such a line that is signed today will save a wrong value again.
- **Onboarding review and account creation read `contracts.installments`** as their source of truth (`lib/operations/onboarding-review.ts` ~307, `lib/mcp/tools/operations.ts` ~340) — only when they run, so correcting a contract afterwards does not change an account already created.
- **Renewals are not in `contracts` for amounts**; the card reads `annual_agreements` by the same token. A renewal whose `cost_summary` layout is not one of the two known ones shows its saved lines and no Edit.
- **`contracts.status` is `signed` on every row** — useless as a filter; `contract_version` is null on most old rows (the line simply omits the version).
- **Filing into CRM storage is a separate matter.** `lib/offers/process-offer-signed.ts` copies the signed PDF into the company's CRM storage only for some cases (and silently skips one: an offer with no `account_id` whose contact already has a company); the card does not depend on it because it reads the original file. Do not backfill into storage "to make the card work".
- **Zsh/Next gotchas hit while building:** a stage with `overflow: hidden` is scrolled by `scrollIntoView` (irrelevant here); the in-app test browser blocks pop-ups opened by automation, so the open button's new tab can only be proven by the route answering 200 with a link that serves `application/pdf`.
