# Signed contracts (the card on the Contact page)
_Last verified against code: 2026-11-13 (after the bug-hunter pass) — Claude (dev job `e65bd6be`, SANDBOX — Antonio ships). **Bug-hunter fixes (same day):** the lines now live in the page (`contact-detail.tsx`), not in the card, so a correction survives a tab switch; a failed company-link/lead lookup stops the load with its real cause instead of hiding lines; a saved correction is never reported as failed when only the read-back fails (`entry: null` + a warning); and when the history row cannot be written the warning shows the old amount and the whole record goes to the server log. **New:** the Contact page's Overview tab has a "Signed contracts" card under the Offers panel: one line per signed offer, with the date signed, the installment amounts SAVED in the system (exactly as saved), a button that opens the signed PDF, and — for the owner — an Edit that corrects a wrong saved amount. Browser-verified on the sandbox with QA test data (formation, onboarding, renewal, tax return, closure; owner and non-owner; empty card; a missing file; a double edit). Before this, no CRM screen linked to a signed contract: the PDFs sat in the private `signed-contracts` bucket and only the client's own pages could open them._

## What it is
Antonio asked for a place on the Contact where he can see the contract the client SIGNED after reading the offer (an offer is only what we offered and sent), click it open, see the installment amounts the system saved at signing, and fix them when they differ from the contract (typically a "." versus "," mix-up). The card reads straight from where the signed files already are — **nothing is copied or moved**.

## Business rules (all approved on the mockup, 2026-11-13)
- **One line per signed OFFER**, newest signing first. If the same offer was signed more than once (12 offers on production have 2-3 `contracts` rows within about a day), only the newest file is listed.
- **A contract shows on a contact** when its offer was made for that person (`offers.contact_id`), was sent to their email (`offers.client_email`, case-insensitive), was made from a lead that became that person (`leads.converted_to_contact_id`), or belongs to a company they are linked to (`offers.account_id` through `account_contacts`). So an annual renewal (it belongs to the company) shows on EVERY person linked to the company.
- **The amounts are shown exactly as saved** — never rounded, never tidied (a saved `2.5001` shows `2.5001`; a saved `200010001000` shows `200,010,001,000`). The yearly figure is the saved `annual_fee`, not January + June, so a record that disagrees with itself is visible. The card does not compare anything and flags nothing: Antonio compares with the PDF himself.
- **Where the amounts come from:** formation / onboarding → `contracts.annual_fee` + `contracts.installments` (`{"jan":n,"jun":n}`, written by the browser signing page). Annual renewal → `annual_agreements.cost_summary` (text such as "$1,000"; the 62 renewal `contracts` rows save NO amount). Tax return, ITIN, closure, name change → "no installment amounts for this contract".
- **Edit is OWNER-ONLY** (`isOwnerOnly`: Antonio, plus `NEXT_PUBLIC_EXTRA_OWNER_EMAILS`). Others see the card without the Edit button, and the route refuses them (403). Open is for all staff (`isStaffUser`); a client, a partner or a signed-out visitor is refused.
- **Both ways of writing an amount are read**: "1,250" and "1.250" are both 1250 (a separator followed by exactly three digits, with 1-3 digits before it, is a thousands mark; one or two digits after it is a decimal). The form shows "Read as: …" before Save; anything else is refused rather than guessed; above 1,000,000 is refused (a glued number).
- **Edit changes the saved amount only.** NEVER the signed PDF, never an invoice already sent, never the company's own installment amounts (`accounts.installment_1/2_amount` — the June cron `annual-installments` uses THOSE, verified in the route's header and select list). For a renewal it rewrites the agreement's `cost_summary` in the layout it already has, and the screen warns that the client's renewal agreement page shows those amounts (`renewal-agreement.tsx` reads `offer.cost_summary`).
- **Every correction is kept**: an `action_log` row (`action_type = signed_contract_amounts_corrected`, `record_id` = the signing record's id for both kinds) with the old text, the new text, both raw values and who. The card shows "Corrected by … on … · was …" from the newest one.
- **No personal data reaches the browser**: the loader selects only the columns the card needs — never `client_passport`, the address or the storage path.

## How it's built
- **Pure logic (unit-tested, client-safe where noted):** `lib/signed-contracts/amount-input.ts` (read "1,250"/"1.250", `formatNumber`, `formatSavedNumber` — client-safe), `contract-amounts.ts` (read/display/build the contract's amounts), `renewal-amounts.ts` (read/rewrite the agreement's `cost_summary`: two real layouts — TWO groups "First Installment (January)" / "Second Installment (June)", or ONE group "<year> Payment Schedule" with an item per installment — anything else is shown as lines and not editable), `labels.ts` (chip + the real agreement titles), `entries.ts` (rows → lines: newest per offer, currency symbol only when the offer says which, `hasUsablePath`), `types.ts`.
- **Server:** `lib/signed-contracts/load.ts` (`loadSignedContractsForContact`, `loadSignedContractEntry`), `lib/signed-contracts/correct.ts` (`correctSignedContractAmounts`).
- **Routes:** `GET /api/crm/signed-contracts/[id]/open` → `{url}` (staff; signs the path RECORDED on `contracts.pdf_path` for 60 s via `createRecordedSignedUrl`, never a folder listing) and `POST /api/crm/signed-contracts/[id]/amounts` `{january, june, expectedUpdatedAt}` → `{entry, warning?}` (owner; `entry` is `null`, with a warning, when the amounts were saved but the line could not be read back; a body that is not an object is refused 400). `[id]` = `contracts.id`.
- **Where the lines are held:** `components/contacts/contact-detail.tsx` keeps them in state (`replaceEntry` in `lib/signed-contracts/list.ts`) and the card is controlled (`entries` + `onEntryCorrected`). The Overview tab is dropped when another tab opens, so state kept inside the card showed the OLD amounts again on coming back (and a second Edit then got 409 from the person's own first edit). Browser-verified: edit → Services tab → Overview shows the corrected amounts and the "Corrected by…" line, and a second Edit saves.
- **Screen:** `components/contacts/signed-contracts-card.tsx`, rendered by `components/contacts/contact-detail.tsx` (Overview tab, in the same grid cell as the Offers panel so they stay together); the data is loaded in `app/(dashboard)/contacts/[id]/page.tsx` and a failure shows a plain note in the card, never a broken page.
- **Safety of the write:** both amounts are parsed on the server with the same function the form uses; the update is matched on the `updated_at` the person saw (`.eq`, or `.is null`), so two people cannot overwrite each other (409 "changed by someone else… reload"); `contracts` has no trigger, so the code sets `updated_at` itself. The contract row written is the newest one for the offer (the line the person is looking at).
- **Open button:** opens a blank tab inside the click, then points it at the private link; if the browser blocks the tab, a "Click here to open the signed contract" link appears (works for a minute); a missing file turns the line amber ("File can't be opened right now").
- **Failure behaviour (never hide, never mislabel):** `load.ts` throws with the real cause when the company-link, lead, offers, contracts or agreements read fails — a person with signed contracts must never see "No signed contract yet" because a lookup timed out (the page shows the error in the card). `correct.ts` reports `ok` once the amounts are written, even if the read-back fails; if the history row cannot be written the warning carries the old amount and the full record goes to `console.error`; reads of the offer / agreement surface their own error (500) instead of a false 404.
- **Tests:** `tests/unit/signed-contracts-amount-input.test.ts`, `-amounts.test.ts`, `-entries.test.ts`, `-routes.test.ts` (route guards, the exact written shape, the old value kept, the 409, renewal rewrite, nothing else written, read-back failure, history-row failure), `-load.test.ts` (the four visibility rules, lookup failures throw, only needed columns read), `-list.test.ts`.

## Gotchas, invariants & past bugs
- **Why the card exists / the mistakes it makes visible.** The signing page computes `annual_fee` / `installments` IN THE BROWSER with `parseFloat(String(raw).replace(/[^0-9.]/g,''))` (`app/offer/[token]/contract/page.tsx` ~663-702, also `service-agreement.tsx`); `sanitizeContractFields` (`lib/offers/public-signing.ts`) only checks the shape. A one-line offer cost like "$2,000/year ($1,000 Jan + $1,000 Jun)" becomes `200010001000`, and an Italian "$2.500/anno ($1.250 gen + $1.250 giu)" becomes `2.5001`. The PDF prints the original text, so the saved record can differ from the contract. **This signing-page bug is NOT fixed by this card** (Antonio chose to see and correct, not to have it investigated); an offer with such a line that is signed today will save a wrong value again.
- **Onboarding review and account creation read `contracts.installments`** as their source of truth (`lib/operations/onboarding-review.ts` ~307, `lib/mcp/tools/operations.ts` ~340) — only when they run, so correcting a contract afterwards does not change an account already created.
- **Renewals are not in `contracts` for amounts**; the card reads `annual_agreements` by the same token. A renewal whose `cost_summary` layout is not one of the two known ones shows its saved lines and no Edit.
- **`contracts.status` is `signed` on every row** — useless as a filter; `contract_version` is null on most old rows (the line simply omits the version).
- **Filing into CRM storage is a separate matter.** `lib/offers/process-offer-signed.ts` copies the signed PDF into the company's CRM storage only for some cases (and silently skips one: an offer with no `account_id` whose contact already has a company); the card does not depend on it because it reads the original file. Do not backfill into storage "to make the card work".
- **Zsh/Next gotchas hit while building:** a stage with `overflow: hidden` is scrolled by `scrollIntoView` (irrelevant here); the in-app test browser blocks pop-ups opened by automation, so the open button's new tab can only be proven by the route answering 200 with a link that serves `application/pdf`.

## How to verify current state
- Unit: `npx vitest run tests/unit/signed-contracts-*.test.ts` (73 tests).
- Contact page (logged in as staff): Overview tab → "SIGNED CONTRACTS" under OFFERS. As an owner each line with amounts has an Edit; as another staff member it does not. `GET /api/crm/signed-contracts/<contracts.id>/open` returns `{url}` and the URL serves `application/pdf`; the bare storage path without the token does not.
- Production numbers when this was built (2026-11-13, read-only): 129 `contracts` rows on 115 offers; 127 reach at least one contact by the rules above, 2 old unlinked offers reach none; 49 rows hold a fee (5 of them wrong in the two ways above); 62 renewals carry their amounts on the agreement.
- Audit of corrections: `select created_at, details->>'by_name', details->>'was_text', details->>'now_text' from action_log where action_type = 'signed_contract_amounts_corrected' order by created_at desc;`
