1. Scope of Service

The Service Provider agrees to manage the change of the legal name of the Client's existing LLC on their behalf. This includes:

  • Collecting up to three (3) proposed new company names from the Client and checking their availability with the Secretary of State of the state where the LLC was formed;
  • Informing the Client which proposed name is available;
  • Preparing and filing the name change form (Articles of Amendment or equivalent) with the Secretary of State;
  • Delivering the official name change document received from the Secretary of State to the Client by email.

This Agreement covers the name change of one (1) LLC only. Notifying the IRS, banks, payment processors and other third parties of the new name is not included unless purchased separately.

2. Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to:

  1. Provide up to three (3) proposed names that comply with the naming rules of the state of formation;
  2. Understand that name availability is decided by the state, and that a name found available may no longer be available at the time of filing;
  3. Update third parties (banks, payment processors, the IRS and others) with the new name, unless this service is purchased separately;
  4. Respond to requests for information within five (5) business days of receiving the request.

3. Service Procedure

The company name change follows these steps:

  1. Step 1 — Proposed Names — The Client provides up to three (3) proposed new names for the LLC. The Service Provider checks their availability in order of preference.
  2. Step 2 — Name Confirmation — The Service Provider informs the Client which name is available. The Client confirms the name to be used before any filing is made.
  3. Step 3 — State Filing — The Service Provider prepares and files the name change form with the Secretary of State. State processing times vary and are outside the Service Provider's control. The LLC keeps its current name until the state approves the change.
  4. Step 4 — Delivery — When the Secretary of State returns the approved name change document, the Service Provider delivers it to the Client by email.

4. Service Provider Commitments

The Service Provider agrees to:

  1. Check the availability of the proposed names with the Secretary of State;
  2. File the name change form within a reasonable timeframe after the Client confirms the new name;
  3. Deliver the official name change document to the Client by email upon receipt from the state.

5. Payment

The total fee for this service is $100, payable in full before the name change work begins. Payment may be made via credit/debit card or bank wire transfer as indicated in the offer.

All fees are non-refundable once the name change work has commenced.

6. Limitation of Liability

The Service Provider’s total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fee paid by the Client. The Service Provider is not liable for delays, rejections or additional fees charged by the Secretary of State, or for consequences resulting from inaccurate, incomplete, or late information provided by the Client.

7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida. Any disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Pinellas County, Florida, under AAA rules.
