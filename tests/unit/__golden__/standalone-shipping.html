1. Scope of Service

The Service Provider agrees to ship documents to the Client, or to the recipient named by the Client, on their behalf. This includes:

  • Preparing the shipment with the documents to be sent;
  • Sending the shipment to the delivery address provided by the Client, with a carrier chosen by the Service Provider;
  • Sending the Client the tracking number by email when the carrier provides one.

This Agreement covers one (1) shipment only. Delivery times given by the carrier are estimates.

2. Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to:

  1. Provide a complete and correct delivery address, including a contact phone number where the carrier requires one;
  2. Make sure someone can receive the shipment at that address;
  3. Pay any customs duties, import taxes or local fees charged by the destination country, if any;
  4. Respond to requests for information within five (5) business days of receiving the request.

3. Service Procedure

The shipment follows these steps:

  1. Step 1 — Preparing the Shipment — The Service Provider prepares the documents and checks the delivery address with the Client.
  2. Step 2 — Shipped — The Service Provider hands the shipment to the carrier and sends the Client the tracking number when available.
  3. Step 3 — Delivered — The shipment is complete when the carrier delivers it to the address provided.

4. Service Provider Commitments

The Service Provider agrees to:

  1. Prepare and send the shipment within a reasonable timeframe after payment and after receiving a complete delivery address;
  2. Send the Client the tracking number by email when the carrier provides one.

5. Payment

The total fee for this service is $100, payable in full before the shipping work begins. Payment may be made via credit/debit card or bank wire transfer as indicated in the offer.

All fees are non-refundable once the shipping work has commenced.

6. Limitation of Liability

The Service Provider’s total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fee paid by the Client. The Service Provider is not liable for delays, loss or damage caused by the carrier or customs, or for consequences resulting from inaccurate, incomplete, or late information provided by the Client.

7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida. Any disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Pinellas County, Florida, under AAA rules.

8. Electronic Signatures

Electronic signatures on this Agreement are valid and binding under the ESIGN Act (15 U.S.C. § 7001) and UETA (Fla. Stat. § 668.50).
