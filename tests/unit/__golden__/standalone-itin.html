1. Scope of Service

The Service Provider agrees to prepare and submit the ITIN application on behalf of the Client. This includes:

  • Preparation of IRS Form W-7 (Application for IRS Individual Taxpayer Identification Number);
  • Preparation of a supporting federal tax return (Form 1040-NR) as required by the IRS;
  • Review and certification by a Certified Acceptance Agent (CAA);
  • Submission of the complete application package to the IRS via certified mail.

This Agreement covers one (1) ITIN application only. Any additional applications or services require a separate agreement.

2. Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to:

  1. Provide accurate and complete personal information, including full legal name, date of birth, country of citizenship, and foreign address;
  2. Provide a valid passport (or certified copy) for identity verification by the Certified Acceptance Agent;
  3. Respond to requests for information within five (5) business days of receiving the request;
  4. Acknowledge processing times are determined by the IRS (typically 7-11 weeks) and are outside the Service Provider's control.

3. Service Procedure

The ITIN application process follows these steps:

  1. Data Collection — The Service Provider sends the Client a secure intake form to collect personal information, passport details, and foreign address.
  2. Document Preparation — The Service Provider prepares Form W-7 and the supporting Form 1040-NR based on the data provided.
  3. CAA Review — A Certified Acceptance Agent reviews and certifies the application, verifying the Client's identity documents (passport).
  4. Submission — The complete application package is sent to the IRS via certified mail with tracking.
  5. Follow-up — The Service Provider monitors the application status. The IRS typically processes ITIN applications within 7-11 weeks.

4. Service Provider Commitments

The Service Provider agrees to:

  1. Prepare the W-7 and 1040-NR forms in compliance with applicable IRS rules and regulations;
  2. Ensure the application is reviewed and certified by a Certified Acceptance Agent (CAA);
  3. Submit the application to the IRS via certified mail with tracking;
  4. Provide the Client with the ITIN number upon receipt from the IRS.

5. Payment

The total fee for this service is $100, payable in full before the ITIN application begins. Payment may be made via credit/debit card or bank wire transfer as indicated in the offer.

All fees are non-refundable once the ITIN application has commenced.

6. Limitation of Liability

The Service Provider’s total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fee paid by the Client. The Service Provider is not liable for the IRS's decision on the application or its processing time, or for consequences resulting from inaccurate, incomplete, or late information provided by the Client.
