1. Scope of Service

The Service Provider agrees to request the update of the legal name on the Client's LLC EIN (Employer Identification Number) account with the IRS. This includes:

  • Preparing the IRS request to update the name on the LLC's EIN account (Form 8822-B, where applicable);
  • Sending the request to the IRS;
  • Following up with the IRS by phone to confirm that the request was received and processed;
  • Informing the Client by email when the IRS has updated the name on the EIN account.

This Agreement covers the EIN account of one (1) LLC only. It does not change the name of the LLC with the Secretary of State, which is a separate service.

2. Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to:

  1. Confirm the exact new legal name of the LLC, as it appears (or will appear) on the Secretary of State record;
  2. Provide the signature and information the IRS requires from the LLC's responsible party;
  3. Acknowledge that processing times and the final decision belong to the IRS, not to the Service Provider;
  4. Respond to requests for information within five (5) business days of receiving the request.

3. Service Procedure

The EIN name change follows these steps:

  1. Step 1 — Preparation — The Service Provider prepares the IRS request using the exact new legal name confirmed by the Client. The responsible party of the LLC signs where the IRS requires it.
  2. Step 2 — Sending to the IRS — The Service Provider sends the signed request to the IRS.
  3. Step 3 — IRS Follow-up — The Service Provider contacts the IRS by phone to confirm receipt and processing. IRS processing times vary and are outside the Service Provider's control.
  4. Step 4 — Confirmation — When the IRS confirms that the name on the EIN account has been updated, the Service Provider informs the Client by email.

4. Service Provider Commitments

The Service Provider agrees to:

  1. Prepare and send the IRS request within a reasonable timeframe after receiving the Client's confirmed new name and any required signature;
  2. Follow up with the IRS until the request is processed or the IRS gives a final answer;
  3. Inform the Client by email of the outcome.

5. Payment

The total fee for this service is $100, payable in full before the EIN name change work begins. Payment may be made via credit/debit card or bank wire transfer as indicated in the offer.

All fees are non-refundable once the EIN name change work has commenced.

6. Limitation of Liability

The Service Provider’s total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fee paid by the Client. The Service Provider is not liable for delays or rejections by the IRS, or for consequences resulting from inaccurate, incomplete, or late information provided by the Client.
